Recording rules: say each line as written, short pause at full stops, two seconds of silence before the sponsor board and before the close.

## Opener (over the hero animation, ~15 s)

1. "This is Steward. A reality layer for industrial agents."
2. "An agent asks: can P-101 restart?"
3. "Steward doesn't guess. It looks, it sends a person if it has to, and it answers with a signed fact."
4. "Let me show you."

## Feature walkthrough (1:25, your own words)

Suggested order, about ten seconds each: Overview with the three scanned factories; the agent eye walking Bristol with green and blue labels; the sump meeting in Conversations; the dispatch SMS and the photo reply; the Compliance page with the regulation and "Ask the agents to fix it"; the owner email with the lawyer in CC and the contractor call; Evals with wait_for and tokens avoided; Tools page with the forged tool if time allows.

Say the sponsor's name once when it is on screen ("that text went out through Inkbox", "this is the server on Manufact", "the row lands in Supabase", "Armature scored it"). I pin the chalk label on that word.

## Sponsor board (~28 s)

1. "Here's how it's built."
2. "My laptop runs the record. SQLite is the truth."
3. "Supabase mirrors it: row-level security decides who may write, the proof ledger is hash-chained, and Realtime feeds the dashboard."
4. "Inkbox gives all seventeen agents an identity. They talk over A2A, email PDFs, and one dispatch line texts and calls real people."
5. "Inkbox's tunnel is the public URL into the laptop."
6. "Manufact hosts the MCP server. Seventy-six tools, one URL, every session captured."
7. "Armature scores every session, and runs our eval suites against the hosted server."
8. "Four products, one loop: ask, look, send a human, sign."
